Before We List

A small investment.
A significantly better first impression.

The property is structurally sound and well-built. A focused pre-listing prep will address the cosmetic and maintenance items that affect how buyers perceive value — and how the home photographs. These are not major renovations; they are targeted fixes that will directly impact offers.

Deep Clean

Full interior and exterior professional cleaning. First impressions are made in the first 30 seconds of a showing and the first photo online.

Palapa & Garden

Palapa maintenance and palm trimming to restore the outdoor space. The back garden is a genuine selling point — it should look the part.

Plumbing & Drainage

Pipe cleaning and septic check to address the drainage odor. Any smell during a showing kills a deal. This is non-negotiable before listing.

Paint Touch-Ups

Ironwork and exterior paint detail work. These small cosmetic finishes signal maintenance and care to buyers evaluating long-distance.

The Right Price,
From Day One.

The previous attempts started too high, then reduced — which signals desperation to buyers without creating urgency. The strategy this time is different: price it correctly from the first day, generate early momentum, and close within 90–120 days.

Why not higher?

The market has already rejected $329K and $262K with extended exposure. The lot at 305 m² limits the upside — comparables with larger land are priced accordingly. $290K creates real buyer urgency without leaving significant money on the table relative to what this property can actually achieve.

Why not lower?

At $272/sqft, this is competitive — not desperate. The location in downtown Todos Santos, the colonial character, and the palapa outdoor space all justify being above the lowest comps in the market.

The first 30 days matter most.

New listings receive their highest visibility in the first 30 days on MLS and digital channels. A correctly priced property generates showings immediately. Price reductions later never recapture that first-impression traffic.
